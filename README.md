# Year 11 Ancient History — lesson site

Lesson pages for Year 11 Ancient History (NSW *Ancient History 11–12 Syllabus* (2024)).

- **Present mode** (projector): arrow keys or space to step through; answers appear one press at a time.
- **Read mode** (phones, or add `?read` to any lesson address): every answer stays hidden until you press its
  "Show" button.

Units so far: Unit 1 · The investigation of ancient sites and sources; Unit 2 · Case study: Persepolis; Unit 3 ·
The treatment and display of human remains; Unit 4 · Case study: Boudicca; Unit 5 · Historical investigation.

Every picture is public domain or openly licensed and is credited on its lesson's last slide. Quotations
are from the editions and translations named on each slide. Outcomes and content: NSW Education Standards
Authority (NESA), *Ancient History 11–12 Syllabus* (2024). © NESA.

These pages are for classroom use. They are not indexed by search engines.
