Women in ancient Greece

Features of ancient societies: Women in ancient Greece (Classical Athens and Ptolemaic Egypt)

Explore women’s family life, work, religion and politics in Classical Athens and Ptolemaic Egypt, through speeches, plays, papyri and women such as Aspasia and Arsinoe II.
